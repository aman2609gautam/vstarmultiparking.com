About Us

VStar Multiparking Systems, headquartered in Mumbai, is a pioneer in multi car parking solutions across India. We specialize in stack, puzzle, tower, and fully automated parking systems for residential, commercial, and industrial projects.

Our mission is to optimize space, enhance safety, and deliver smart, efficient parking solutions with innovation and reliability.

With years of experience, our team designs, installs, and maintains parking systems that suit the unique requirements of every project.
